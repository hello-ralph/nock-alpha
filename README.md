# Nock

**Nock, an offline content automation production studio.**

Nock is a Windows desktop app by [Humind Creatives](https://www.humindcreatives.com) that turns a monthly social calendar spreadsheet into finished, on-brand designs. It uses your own design templates and an AI model stored on your computer, so it works with no internet connection and no cloud subscription.

> **The free private alpha is open.** We're inviting a small group of testers to use Nock for free and tell us what works and what doesn't. [Join the alpha](#join-the-free-private-alpha)

---

## Why Nock

- **Offline by design.** Your copy, templates and client work stay on your machine. Nothing is sent to someone else's servers.
- **Local AI.** The AI model is stored and runs on your computer. No per-image credits, no monthly AI bill.
- **Your templates, filled for you.** Map a template once, and every row of your calendar becomes a finished design in its own post folder.
- **Built by a designer.** Nock comes from a brand identity studio that produces social content every month and wanted a faster way to do it.

## How it works

1. **Upload your social calendar.** Column names don't matter. Nock reads each column by what it contains (copy, dates, keywords, sizes).
2. **Map a template.** Tell Nock which part of the design is the headline, the caption and so on.
3. **Run the calendar.** Each row becomes a post folder with a filled design, a preview and a notes file.
4. **Review and approve.** Check every export, ask the local AI for new copy where needed, and re-run.

## What works today

- Social calendar upload and month view
- Post folders and previews for every row
- Static posts through Adobe Illustrator
- PDF carousels
- Animated posts through Adobe After Effects
- Word documents
- Long-form page layouts from InDesign files (preview and fill)

More apps are scripted and being tested. See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

## Who it's for

- Social media and content teams producing a month of posts at a time
- Designers and small studios who want to automate repeat layouts
- Teams that can't send client work to online tools

Nock also includes a built-in Creative Studio for people without Adobe.
