# Nock roadmap

What's working, what's next, and what's further out. Plans can change based on what alpha testers tell us.

## Working now

- Social calendar upload, month view and post folders
- Static posts through Adobe Illustrator
- PDF carousels
- Animated posts through Adobe After Effects
- Word documents
- InDesign page layouts: read, preview and fill, with extra pages added when the copy runs long
- Review flow: pending, under review, new copy from the local AI, approved
- Built-in Creative Studio for people without Adobe

## Next

- Faster AI replies in the dashboard
- Proven exports from Photoshop, InDesign and the Office apps (PowerPoint, Excel)
- Windows installer for alpha testers
- Image checks in copy documents, with a warning for low-resolution images

## Later

- Canva connection
- Template store with ready-made designs
- More AI model options for different computers
