# Nock changelog

Progress notes, newest first.

## November 2026

- Opened the free private alpha and this public page for testers.
- Long-form page layouts: Nock now reads InDesign files without InDesign installed, matches copy to the right frames by type size, and adds pages when the copy runs longer than the template.
- Page preview shows exactly where each piece of copy will land before anything is saved.
- Social calendar runs produce Illustrator static posts, PDF carousels, After Effects animated posts and Word documents.
